WHY WORK WITH US

A team that cares about the whole chain.

We are small enough that your work matters and global enough that it travels far. Here is what you can expect.

Work that travels

Your work reaches kitchens and shelves across the globe. Sourcing, quality and logistics all connect to buyers in many markets.

See the whole chain

We are vertically integrated, so you learn the business from the farm gate to the final delivery, not just one corner of it.

Rooted in good practice

We work with certified organic farmer groups and fair-trade partners. The sustainability goals are part of the job, not a poster.

Real ownership

A focused team where your ideas ship. You will own projects early and see the result of your work quickly.

Room to grow

Learn across sourcing, food technology, compliance and trade. We back your growth with mentoring and time to learn.

A team that backs you

Direct, kind and curious colleagues. We celebrate good work, share credit and look after each other.
