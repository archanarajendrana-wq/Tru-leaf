3. Goals, Non-goals & Success Metrics

What this is: what success looks like, in numbers, and what you are choosing not to chase. Non-goals stop scope creep; metrics are how you will know it worked.

Goals

  • Business: grow repeat orders by 20% within 6 months of launch.
  • User: reorder a usual basket in under 60 seconds.

Non-goals

Things we are deliberately not optimizing for. Different from out of scope: non-goals are priorities, out of scope is excluded work.

  • Not optimizing for first-time discovery of new products.
  • Not replacing the web store in this release.
