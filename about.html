Where it began

From Food Processing Engineer to Entrepreneur, Driven by a Deep-Rooted Passion for Food Processing, Food Safety & Innovation.

In 2018, Renious Charles and John Wesley, two Food Processing Engineers, saw a gap between India’s rich food potential and what global buyers expected: consistent quality, food safety, traceability and reliable supply.

With experience across food processing, quality systems, certification, food safety and farm-level sourcing, they understood the challenges from within the industry.

That led to the creation of Truleaf Foods, built not simply to trade food products, but to verify, source and deliver them with confidence.

Today, we connect trusted Indian producers and processors with buyers in India and international markets, combining food science, food safety and supply-chain discipline.

From source to shipment, we build food supply with confidence.

From India to the world

India has an extraordinary agricultural and food-processing ecosystem. Our ambition is to connect that strength with buyers who expect international standards of quality, consistency and compliance.

Today, Truleaf Foods works across fresh and processed fruits and vegetables, coconut products, frozen foods, preserved and acidified products.

It is about moving quality, confidence and trust with every shipment.

How we work

A structured approach, from sourcing to delivery.

Rooted in Southern Tamil Nadu and backed by 10+ years of industry experience, Truleaf Foods combines Food Engineering knowledge with a practical understanding of sourcing, quality, and regulatory requirements. We connect suppliers and markets through dependable relationships, thoughtful product selection, and a commitment to responsible trade.

Responsible Sourcing

We work to identify suitable suppliers for fresh fruits and vegetables, raw materials, and ingredients, considering product specifications, availability, quality expectations, and customer requirements.

Quality with Technical Understanding

Our Food Engineering foundation helps us understand product characteristics, quality parameters, specifications, and relevant regulatory requirements when evaluating sourcing opportunities.

Trade & Supply Coordination

We coordinate with suppliers and customers on product requirements, commercial terms, documentation, and logistics to support smooth domestic and international trade.

Our principles
